The project

AI Study Material Assistant

This learning project explores how Generative AI can help students work with their own notes. The idea is to let a student ask questions about study materials and receive relevant explanations grounded in the information retrieved from those materials.

📚

Study-focused

Designed around learning materials, revision notes, and student questions.

🔎

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Explores retrieving relevant text chunks before generating an answer, helping connect responses to supplied materials.

💬

Natural-language questions

Aims to make it easier to ask questions and get clear explanations without searching through every page manually.

Current status: This is a student learning prototype under development. Features and results may change as I continue learning and testing.
How it works

From notes to useful answers

A simple RAG workflow

  1. Prepare study content and split it into smaller text chunks.
  2. Represent text as embeddings so related content can be compared.
  3. Retrieve the chunks most relevant to a student's question.
  4. Send the question and retrieved context to a language model to generate an answer.
